# CreativeWriter — AI Writing Studio with Agentic AI That Knows Your Story

**Try it free:** [creativewriter.dev](https://creativewriter.dev) · 7-day trial, no credit card

---

Most AI writing tools just take your prompt and run with it. You end up copy-pasting character descriptions, plot summaries, and scene context into every single prompt.

**CreativeWriter does it differently.** You build your story's world once — characters, locations, factions, plot notes — and the AI uses all of it automatically. Every time.

![CreativeWriter story list](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MarcoDroll/creativewriter-public/main/outreach/screenshots/storylist-desktop.png)
*Your stories at a glance — create new stories, import existing projects, or generate cover images.*

---

## 🤖 Agentic AI — The Core Feature

CreativeWriter's agentic mode is an AI agent that **actively researches your story during generation**. It reads your outline, looks up relevant codex entries, and pulls in prior scenes — all on its own. If your character got a scar in chapter 3, the AI knows about it in chapter 12. No manual context management needed.

![Agentic AI generation streaming prose in real time](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MarcoDroll/creativewriter-public/main/outreach/screenshots/beat-input-agentic-generation.png)
*Agentic generation reads your outline, codex, and prior scenes to write contextually aware prose — streamed in real time.*

## ✍️ Beat-Level AI Writing

Instead of generating walls of text, the AI works at the beat level. Write a brief description, generate full prose, rewrite with different instructions, or restore from version history. You stay in control of every story beat.
